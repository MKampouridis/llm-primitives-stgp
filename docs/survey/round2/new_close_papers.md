# Close papers: what their methods actually do (read 2026-10-30)

Context: novelty check for the GP extension of "Large Language Quant" (Clayman, Kampouridis & Papastylianou, CIFEr 2026).
Categories: (A) LLM-derived primitives used as GP terminals/functions; (B) LLM solutions seeding a GP population; (C) LLM as the variation/repair operator; (D) co-evolution, where an LLM grows, prunes or refines the primitive set between or during GP runs, or GP evolves specifications that the LLM implements.

Method: I downloaded the full arXiv HTML of all 7 papers with curl, converted it to text with the math kept, and read the method, experiment and appendix sections. For InsightSR I also checked the PDF, because the HTML drops its discussion paragraph. Metadata comes from the arXiv abstract pages. Quotes are kept to under one short sentence.

---

## 1. EvoQuantLab (arXiv:2610.17130) — HIGHEST PRIORITY

**a) Bibliographic details.** Haolin Yang, Zhiqiang Qian, Chutian Chen, Jipeng Zhang, Zhenghao Zhu, Tangjia Deng, Jian Xie, Shuaishuai Gong, Yike Guo, Sirui Han (HKUST, Microsoft, Tsinghua, U. Macau). "EvoQuantLab: Training-Free Dual-Loop Evolution for Auditable Quantitative Research". v1 12 Oct 2026, v2 14 Oct 2026 (I read v2). cs.AI preprint; no venue, comments or journal reference given. I read the full text, including Appendices A–G.

**b) Representation and genuine GP.**
- Individuals are **typed symbolic factor expression trees**, drawn from the expression space generated by the active terminal set T_c and the typed operator set O_c of cycle c.
- This is a **genuine, code-driven tree GP**:
  - tournament selection;
  - typed subtree crossover;
  - point mutation over valid grammar nodes;
  - deduplication (syntactic duplicates and highly correlated expressions are replaced by "targeted immigrants" from under-represented families);
  - a family-stratified elite archive.
- Fitness is the worst development-fold metric minus λ times tree complexity (size and depth).
- The LLM does **not** perform per-candidate variation. The paper stresses that a deterministic, budget-accounted GP engine does the search.
- Example evolved factor (Crypto, Appendix D.1): add(add(ticket24, top5m168(Δ72(book_frame_coverage168))), sjv168(cci168)).
- GP hyperparameters (population size, generations, rates) are **not reported**.

**c) Where primitives come from.**
- The L1 "Factory" instantiates typed, "economically grounded" terminals covering order flow, funding, microstructure and momentum.
- **Knowledge ingestion (L1):**
  - Economic mechanisms are extracted from academic papers, financial disclosures and analyst reports into structured "Reference Cards". The case study calls this a "model-assisted L1 pathway".
  - Proposed primitives enter an isolated proposal queue with status PROPOSED_NOT_ACTIVE.
  - To be admitted across a cycle boundary, a primitive must pass a pointwise reference check, a prefix-causality (look-ahead) test and, in the case study, a pre-registered paired-seed counterfactual against a time-shifted control.
- **Terminal lifecycle (Appendix F):**
  - Each active terminal carries an evidence tuple: utilisation, candidate survival, residual contribution, cross-fold stability.
  - The possible states are active, interaction-only, dormant and banned.
  - A terminal is banned only for leakage, false field semantics or repeated falsification.
  - Dormant terminals stay in the manifest.
- **Changes happen between cycles, not within one.** Loop A emits lifecycle and budget decisions. The outer Loop B is an LLM reviewer: Gate 1 is an overfitting scorecard, and Gate 2 is a routed review that proposes bounded "patches" to the next protocol version. That version includes the terminal set, the operator set, budgets and evaluation rules.
  - Example (D.1): after a failed cycle, the child policy introduced 15 "cross-layer terminal bans".
  - Example (D.3): an LLM-proposed terminal (book_dimb524) was rejected and then banned or removed in the next cycle.
- **Ambiguity 1 — what a primitive is.** The terminals behave like named, precomputed feature series, such as book_frame_coverage168 and cci168. The paper does **not** say whether the LLM writes executable code for new terminals or operators, or only specifies them (formula or mechanism) for a deterministic factory to implement.
- **Ambiguity 2 — operators.** The ablation "No L1 search" freezes "terminal and operator spaces" and mentions "dynamic operator retrieval across cycles". However, the text describes no mechanism by which the LLM creates new operators. The documented lifecycle covers terminals only.
- **Who decides.** Admission and lifecycle use deterministic evidence plus LLM-proposed patches, which are admitted only if they cite evidence, have a validation plan and a regression test, and touch no immutable field.

**d) LLM roles mapped to categories.**
- **A — yes, partially.** An LLM-assisted pathway proposes new terminals from literature. After empirical admission tests, these become terminals that the typed GP composes. It is not stated whether the LLM writes the terminal code.
- **B — unclear.** The paper says it "combines LLM-generated economic hypotheses" with GP, and Appendix A lists "candidate and patch proposals made by the frozen API model". It never explains how, or whether, LLM-proposed candidates seed the GP population.
- **C — no.** The GP operators are purely syntactic and run by code.
- **D — yes.** Between cycles, an LLM reviewer (Loop B) plus evidence-driven lifecycle rules add, ban, make dormant or re-budget terminals and patch the protocol for the next cycle. This is the closest match to "the LLM revises the primitive set between cycles". The criteria are usage and fitness evidence plus LLM judgement, gated by deterministic tests.

**e) Output and how it becomes trades.**
- The output is **cross-sectional alpha factors** (next-day or next-hour cross-sectional return prediction), scored by IC, ICIR, worst-fold dev metric and others. The GP output is not a per-asset entry/exit rule.
- **Equities:** admitted factors are pre-screened and fed as features to a **LightGBM** ranker. Its scores become dollar-neutral long-short weights (w ∝ s − mean(s)).
- **Crypto:** factor-level rank signals are inverse-volatility scaled and smoothed over 72 hours, then averaged equal-weight at the position level, with hourly rebalancing and 1-hour latency.

**f) Domain and data.**
- Markets: CSI 500 and S&P 500 (daily OHLCV panels) and hourly crypto (cross-section with participant-flow terminals). A Hong Kong limit-order-book adapter is described in Appendix G but no results are reported for it.
- Train/dev periods:
  - equities: 2025-03-31 to 2026-04-30;
  - crypto: 2025-04-01 to 2026-03-31.
- Out-of-sample periods are very short:
  - CSI 500: 2026-05-06 to 2026-08-17;
  - S&P 500: 2026-05-01 to 2026-08-14;
  - crypto: 2026-04-01 to 2026-06-09.
- Costs: equity results are explicitly **gross** (before fees and slippage). Crypto results are **net** of 5 bps per one-way turnover, excluding funding.
- Baselines (AlphaAgent, QuantaAlpha, FactorMiner, RD-Agent) all use Claude Opus 5.
- The run took 22 h, with 50 LLM calls costing about $9.

**g) Advanced-mathematics primitives.** **None.** Terminal families are order flow, funding, microstructure, momentum, spread, coverage, CCI, RSI and CLV. There are no information-theory, information-geometry, optimal-transport, rough-path, Hurst/fractal or statistical-physics primitives. "Entropy" appears only as a population-diversity diagnostic (normalised family entropy), not as a primitive.

## 2. LineageEvo (arXiv:2610.29663)

**a) Bibliographic details.** Xintong Liu, Chenkai Zhang, Yanzhao Ma, Peng Yang (SUSTech). "Lineage-Aware Search Control for LLM-Based Evolutionary Factor Mining". v1 21 Oct 2026. q-fin.CP; no venue. Full text read, including the prompts appendix.

**b) Representation and variation.**
- Individuals are formulaic alpha **expressions in a fixed Qlib-style DSL** (untyped from the user's perspective; variables, constants, operators such as TsSkew and TsCov).
- **The LLM performs all variation.** Mutation and crossover are prompts that ask the LLM for a local leaf or subtree edit, or an asymmetric subtree transplant.
- Code is used only for deterministic validation (grammar, executability, duplicates, NaN), evaluation, lineage-DAG bookkeeping, lineage-stratified parent selection (LSPS) and the elite archive.
- There is **no code-level tree crossover or mutation.**

**c) Primitives.** The DSL operators and market variables are **fixed by humans**; the list is omitted in the paper's appendix. They are never added or removed. What the LLM rewrites are the natural-language "priors" (lineage and global mutation/crossover experience), triggered by IC improvement or degradation thresholds.

**d) LLM roles mapped to categories.**
- **B — LLM-generated population, but not a GP.** There are 10 LLM-generated seed roots, but the population being seeded is LLM-EA, not GP.
- **C — yes.** The LLM is the mutation and crossover operator.
- **A — no.**
- **D — no** (only prompt-guidance priors evolve).

**e) Output.** Cross-sectional factors scored by IC (Spearman), with ICIR. The top-20 factors are z-scored, combined with equal weight and traded with the Qlib TopK-Dropout strategy (K=50, drop 5, daily).

**f) Domain and data.**
- CSI 500 and S&P 500, daily. Train 2015–2020, validation 2021-01 to 2022-04, test 2022-05 to 2026-04.
- Budget: 200 valid evaluations; deepseek-v4-flash.
- Costs are **net**: open 0.15%, close 0.25%, minimum 5.

**g) Advanced-mathematics primitives.** None; standard time-series and statistical operators.

## 3. TreEvo (arXiv:2508.16334)

**a) Bibliographic details.** Junji Ren, Junjie Zhao, Shengcai Liu, Peng Yang. "From Flat to Hierarchical: Evolving Tree-structured Thoughts for Fine-grained Alpha Mining". v1 22 Aug 2025 (titled "From Linear to Hierarchical…"); v2 31 May 2026 (I read v2). cs.CE; I found no venue. Full text read, including prompts.

**b) Representation and variation.**
- Each individual is a **natural-language "thought" organised as an ASCII tree**: root = financial objective, internal nodes = statistical operations, leaves = OHLCV/amount data.
- **For evaluation**, the LLM translates each thought into executable Python code.
- **All five operators are LLM prompts:**
  - crossover (sub-idea transplant);
  - Mutation-R (whole new tree);
  - Mutation-I (internal subtree);
  - Mutation-F (leaf parameter);
  - pruning.
- **No code-level GP.** Selection is (N+N) truncation, with N=10 and a 200-evaluation budget, using Qwen3-Max.

**c) Primitives.** The prompt lists suggested node vocabularies: semantic nodes; operations such as rolling_mean, zscore, regression_beta; and data leaves. LLM-driven EAs are explicitly given "no restriction" on operators. Nothing is added or removed by any criterion. The 22-operator fixed set applies only to the GP and RL baselines.

**d) LLM roles mapped to categories.**
- **C — yes** (all variation).
- **B — LLM initialises the population**, but not a GP's.
- **D — no** in the categorised sense. The "specification → implementation" split (thought → code) exists, but the specifications are evolved by the LLM, not by GP.
- **A — no.**

**e) Output.** A single best cross-sectional alpha, scored by IC/RankIC against 5-day forward returns. There is an illustrative Top-50/Drop-5 backtest on CSI300.

**f) Domain and data.**
- CSI300, CSI500, SPX, NDX, daily, using OHLCV + vwap.
- Train 2016–2019, validation 2020, test 2021–2023; plus a quarterly walk-forward on 2023.
- Transaction costs are **not stated**.

**g) Advanced-mathematics primitives.** None.

## 4. InsightSR (arXiv:2608.25291)

**a) Bibliographic details.** Yating Ling, Wenjing Cun, Zhitang Chen. "InsightSR: Refining Symbolic Regression Search Spaces via Parallel Semantic and Structural LLM Guidance". v1 26 Aug 2026. cs.LG; no venue stated (the PDF uses an AAAI-style layout). Full text read, HTML plus PDF.

**b) Representation and variation.** Individuals are expression trees in **PySR** — a genuine GP with code-driven mutation and crossover over a fixed operator set. The LLM sits around the GP as a "guiding layer" and performs no per-individual variation.

**c) Primitives.**
- **Structural Feature Pathway.** Each outer generation, the LLM proposes nonlinear transforms φ(x), such as x^n and sin(x). These are appended as **precomputed columns** (X_aug), i.e. new **terminals**, and they **accumulate** across generations.
- After each PySR run, an LLM "critic" labels each engineered feature as effective or ineffective by its residual-error contribution. The labels are stored in a knowledge base that "prioritises" features in the next round.
- **Explicit removal of features is not described.**
- The LLM also supplies **per-operator complexity biases** that make human-fixed PySR operators cheaper or dearer. It does not create new operators.

**d) LLM roles mapped to categories.**
- **A — yes**, as precomputed feature terminals rather than executable functions or operators.
- **B — yes.** The Semantic Seed Pathway supplies dimensionally consistent skeletons that seed the PySR population; the best candidate from the previous round is kept.
- **C — no.**
- **D — yes, partially.** In an outer loop of 3–30 LLM generations, the LLM grows the terminal set and re-weights operators based on its own judgement of feature utility. Pruning is not explicit.

**e) Output.** Scientific symbolic-regression equations (MSE, R², accuracy within tolerance).

**f) Domain and data.** Feynman, LLM-SRBench and LLM-SR real-world datasets (oscillators, E. coli, stress–strain). **Not finance.** LLM: Qwen-3.5 27B.

**g) Advanced-mathematics primitives.** None. The paper does use physical dimensional analysis.

## 5. EvoMO-SR (arXiv:2609.36187)

**a) Bibliographic details.** Cristina Rossetti (Politecnico di Torino), Anna V. Kononova, Thomas Bäck, Niki van Stein (LIACS Leiden), Fei Liu (UZH/ETH). "EvoMO-SR: Multiobjective LLM-based Evolution of Symbolic Expressions with substructure guidance". v1 28 Sep 2026. cs.LG preprint; no venue. Full text read, including the appendix.

**b) Representation and variation.**
- Individuals are **Python/NumPy equation skeletons**, with constants fitted by L-BFGS-B.
- **The LLM performs all variation**, through two mutation prompts: "refine" or "propose new". There is **no crossover and no code GP.**
- Survival uses NSGA-II-style non-dominated sorting on NMSE versus AST node count, with crowding distance and a Pareto archive.

**c) Primitives.**
- There is no fixed primitive set.
- A **substructure archive** (size 10) holds additive subtrees that are new in an offspring relative to its parent. They are extracted by code using SymPy canonicalisation.
- A new substructure replaces the worst one if its offspring's NMSE is lower.
- With p=0.5, a random archived substructure is injected into the mutation prompt as something to include. These are **reusable building blocks suggested via the prompt**, not terminals composed by a GP.

**d) LLM roles mapped to categories.**
- **B/C — yes, but for an LLM-EA, not a GP.** The LLM initialises and mutates.
- The archive is code-curated, so it is not D.
- **A — no.**

**e) Output.** Scientific symbolic-regression equations.

**f) Domain and data.** LSR-Synth (129 problems), oscillators and random functions. **Not finance.** Main LLM: Llama-3.1-8B.

**g) Advanced-mathematics primitives.** None.

## 6. LLM-GP-BT (arXiv:2502.07772)

**a) Bibliographic details.** Azizjon Kobilov, Jianglin Lan (University of Glasgow). "Automatic Robot Task Planning by Integrating Large Language Model with Genetic Programming". v1 11 Feb 2025. cs.RO; arXiv comment "Submitted to IEEE Conference". Full text read.

**b) Representation and variation.** Individuals are **behaviour trees** (Sequence/Fallback/Parallel control nodes over robot action and condition leaves). This is a **genuine GP**: 40% crossover, 60% mutation (node mutation, addition, deletion), 10% elitism, tournament selection, 8000 generations. The GP set-up and fitness function are reused from earlier GP-BT work.

**c) Primitives.** **Fixed by humans**: robot skills (Localise, Pick, Place, Move to TableX, …) and condition checks. The LLM never changes them.

**d) LLM roles mapped to categories.**
- **B — yes, purely.** GPT-4o (multimodal, using environment images) generates BTs. Invalid ones are regenerated, and only those above a fitness threshold form the initial GP population (30).
- **Repair — loosely.** The LLM regenerates trees that fail node validation, but only before the GP starts.
- **A, C, D — no.**

**e) Output.** A robot task plan (BT) for a pick-and-place simulation. **Not finance.**

**f) Domain and data.** Simulated office environment; deterministic and stochastic failure probabilities.

**g) Advanced-mathematics primitives.** None.

## 7. CogAlpha (arXiv:2511.18850) — feature-family check only

**a) Bibliographic details.** Fengyuan Liu, Yi Huang, Sichun Luo, Yuqi Wang, Yazheng Yang, Xinye Li, Zefa Hu, Junlan Feng, Qi Liu. "Cognitive Alpha Mining via LLM-Driven Code-Based Evolution". v1 Nov 2025 through v4 11 Jul 2026. **ACL 2026** (Long Papers), pp. 11715–11749, according to the ACL Anthology. I read the full text of the current arXiv version, focusing on the agent hierarchy and appendices.

**Agent hierarchy.** There are 21 LLM agents in seven levels, working from OHLCV only:
1. market cycle, volatility regime;
2. tail risk, crash predictor;
3. liquidity, order imbalance, price–volume coherence, volume structure;
4. trend, reversal, range volatility, lag response, volatility asymmetry;
5. **"Multi-Scale Complexity": AgentDrawdown, AgentFractal**;
6. regime gating, stability;
7. **"Geometric & Fusion"**: bar shape (candlestick geometry), creative, composite, herding.

**Advanced-mathematics families.**
- **Fractal and long memory: yes, at the level of description.** AgentFractal targets "multi-scale roughness and long-memory characteristics" via cross-horizon variability. The Hurst exponent, DFA and R/S are not named anywhere in the text.
- **"Geometry"** means candlestick morphology (body, shadow, symmetry) and drawdown–recovery "geometry". It is **not** information geometry.
- **"Fisher"** in generated alphas (factor_pressure_drawdown_fisher_10d, factor_herd_fisher_20d_rollingwinsor) is the **Fisher z-transform** (arctanh of a rolling correlation). It is **not** Fisher information or the Fisher–Rao metric.
- **"Energy"** appears loosely ("drawdown energy", "energy alignment", "cyclical energy, hidden harmonics" in the market-cycle prompt).
- **Not found anywhere in the text:** entropy, information theory, Wasserstein or optimal transport, rough-path signatures, information geometry, statistical-physics phase transitions.

**Method, briefly.** Individuals are Python alpha code. The LLM performs all variation (Mutation Agent, Crossover Agent), and a multi-agent quality checker repairs code. Alphas are fed to LightGBM and backtested with Qlib top-50/drop-5, with excess return net of cost_t. Data: CSI300, CSI500, S&P500, HSI, HSCI.

## Comparative summary

Target mechanism: **LLM-generated executable primitives, composed by a typed GP, with the LLM revising the primitive set between cycles, applied to trading/alpha.**

| Paper | Genuine (typed) GP? | LLM-generated primitives? | LLM revises primitive set between cycles? | Finance? | Advanced-maths primitives? | Per-asset entry/exit rules? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| EvoQuantLab | **Yes, typed tree GP** | **Yes**: LLM-assisted literature extraction proposes terminals; executable status/authorship unclear | **Yes**: Loop B patches, terminal bans/dormancy/admission by evidence + paired tests | **Yes** (cross-sectional alpha) | No | No: cross-sectional factors → LightGBM / long-short |
| LineageEvo | No (LLM-EA) | No (fixed DSL) | No (only prompt priors) | Yes (alpha) | No | No |
| TreEvo | No (LLM-EA on NL thoughts) | No | No | Yes (alpha) | No | No |
| InsightSR | Yes (PySR, untyped) | Yes, but precomputed feature columns plus operator cost biases | Partly: features accumulate, LLM labels utility; no explicit pruning | No (scientific SR) | No | n/a |
| EvoMO-SR | No (LLM-EA) | Substructures, code-extracted, injected via prompt | No (archive is code-curated by NMSE) | No | No | n/a |
| LLM-GP-BT | Yes (BT GP) | No (fixed robot skills) | No | No (robotics) | No | n/a |
| CogAlpha | No (LLM-EA on code) | n/a | No | Yes (alpha) | Fractal/long-memory only, by description; "Fisher" = z-transform | No |

**Conclusion.** **EvoQuantLab is the only paper that substantially performs the combination.** It is a typed tree GP for alpha factors. It has a cross-cycle outer loop in which an LLM reviewer, together with deterministic evidence, admits, bans or makes dormant terminals and patches the operator, terminal and budget protocol. It also has an LLM-assisted pathway that proposes new terminals from the literature, tested before admission.

What EvoQuantLab lacks relative to the planned paper:
1. It does not show that the LLM **writes the executable implementation** of primitives. Proposals are "reference cards", and terminals look like named precomputed features. Creation of new operators is not described.
2. Its output is **cross-sectional alpha factors**, scored by IC/IR and turned into portfolios via LightGBM or equal-weight long-short. It does not produce per-asset boolean entry/exit trading rules.
3. It has **no advanced-mathematics primitive families**: no entropy, rate-distortion, signatures, Fisher–Rao, Wasserstein, Hurst or phase transitions.
4. Its primitive sourcing is **literature/mechanism extraction**, not LLM-authored mathematical code.
5. Its evaluation is very short (3–3.5-month OOS, about 13-month train/dev), and equity results are gross of costs.

What each other paper lacks:
- **InsightSR** has the closest *mechanism* outside finance: GP plus LLM-grown terminal set across outer rounds plus LLM seeding. But its primitives are precomputed feature columns rather than executable operators, removal is not explicit, the GP is untyped, and it is not finance.
- **LineageEvo, TreEvo, EvoMO-SR and CogAlpha** have the LLM do all variation (category C) with no code-level GP and no LLM-managed primitive set.
- **LLM-GP-BT** is pure category B (LLM seeding of a GP) in robotics.
- **CogAlpha** covers fractal/long-memory only at the level of agent descriptions. It has no information-geometry, optimal-transport, rough-path or entropy primitives, and its "Fisher" is the Fisher z-transform.
